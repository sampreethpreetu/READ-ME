### 📌 Featured Project

- **Sales Dashboard – ShopNest E-Commerce Analytics**

**Code:** [ShopNest Dashboard.pbix](https://github.com/sampreethpreetu)

**Goal:** To analyze e-commerce order, delivery, and payment performance and identify which product categories, payment methods, and operational factors drive revenue and customer satisfaction.

**Description:** The project analyzed ~99,000 orders and 112,650 line items across a 7-table relational model (Orders, Order Items, Customers, Payments, Reviews, Products, Sellers) spanning September 2016–October 2018. Work involved modeling relationships across tables, creating 20+ DAX measures, and designing a 10-page interactive report with slicers and drill-downs across month/quarter/year, state, and product category.

**Skills:** data modeling, DAX, KPI design, dashboard/report design, trend analysis.

**Technology:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query.

**Results:** The top 10 product categories contributed 63% of total revenue. 7.9% of orders were delivered late, highlighting a logistics improvement opportunity. Credit cards accounted for 74% of all payments, informing checkout and finance prioritization.
